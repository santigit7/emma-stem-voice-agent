AUTOCAD COMMAND REFERENCE
LINE (L): draw straight line segments.
CIRCLE (C): draw a circle by center and radius or diameter.
OFFSET (O): create a parallel copy of a line, polyline, or curve at a set distance.
TRIM (TR): cut objects back to a cutting edge.
EXTEND (EX): lengthen objects to meet a boundary.
FILLET (F): round or square off the corner between two lines. Radius 0 makes a sharp corner.
MOVE (M) and COPY (CO): relocate or duplicate objects using a base point.
ROTATE (RO) and SCALE (SC): turn or resize objects around a base point.
HATCH (H): fill a closed area with a pattern.
LAYER (LA): open the layer manager.
DIMLINEAR (DLI): add a horizontal or vertical dimension.
EXTRUDE (EXT): turn a closed 2D shape into a 3D solid by giving it height.
PRESSPULL (PRESS): push or pull a bounded area to add or remove 3D volume.

FUSION 360 BASICS
Sketch (S), Line (L), Rectangle (R), Circle (C), Offset (O), Trim (T), Dimension (D).
Extrude (E): give a sketch profile thickness. Fillet (F): round edges.
Press Pull (Q): push or pull faces directly.
Workflow: sketch, constrain and dimension, extrude, then add fillets and holes.
